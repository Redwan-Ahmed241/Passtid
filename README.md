## The problem

If you are Swedish and live in the UK, getting a passport, a national ID card or a coordination number starts with an appointment in London. Times are scarce. They are taken quickly, they can appear at any hour, and finding one often means checking the calendar again and again between work, school and everything else.

Families have it harder, because everyone who needs a document has to be there in person.

## What Passtid does about it

You tell us once which days and times you can attend. Passtid keeps watching for you, and when a time fits, you either get an alert and book it yourself, or have it booked for you.

- **No more refreshing.** The watching happens around the clock.
- **Only times you can make.** Anything outside your dates is ignored.
- **One request for the whole family.** Add everyone who needs an appointment.
- **In English or Swedish.**

A slot or a successful booking cannot be guaranteed. It depends on what is released.
